Frequently Asked Questions
How do I accept a booking request?
Go to My Bookings → find the booking with "Pending Confirmation" status → tap Accept. You have 24 hours from the time the request was made.
When does my payout get released?
Payout is released when you tap "Confirm Dispatch" in a booking. The net amount (gross minus CCX fee and TDS) is transferred to your bank within 2–3 business days.
My facility is under review for more than 72 hours. What should I do?
Raise a support ticket with your Application ID (CCX-STGE-XXXX) and our team will check the review queue. Typical review time is 48–72 hours on business days.
